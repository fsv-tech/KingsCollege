Academic Outcomes

Driving Exceptional Attainment Through Specialist Teaching

Academic distinction resides at the very core of all we do at King’s College Doha. Our students thrive within an inspirational and supportive environment where Outstanding teaching, personalized attention, and high expectations are seamlessly integrated to foster the highest achievement in every child.

We firmly believe that every student is capable of flourishing. Our talented, British-trained teachers actively cultivate curiosity, build resilience, and instil a passion for lifelong learning, empowering students to explore their unique strengths and face any challenge with courage and confidence.

Below, you can review how our pupils are achieving exceptional academic outcomes — a powerful reflection of their commitment, perseverance, and the dedicated support of our teaching team.

More than Double the UK Average

Outstanding GCSE Achievement: Pupils achieved a remarkable percentage of Grade 9s — more than double the UK national average of 5%, demonstrating exceptional attainment across subjects.

98% Pass Rate

Consistent Excellence: 98% of our pupils achieved a pass grade at GCSE, reflecting their hard work, determination, and the unwavering support of their teachers.

2x UK Value-Added Progress

Exceptional Value-Added Results: Our pupils’ progress once again exceeds that of the UK average by more than double, highlighting the strength of our personalised learning approach.

100% University Offers

A Level Success: All pupils received offers for their chosen universities, with a 100% pass rate across A levels, AS levels, and BTEC Level 3 qualifications.
