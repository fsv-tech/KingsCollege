Join King’s College Doha

We are delighted you are ready to take the next step towards joining the King’s College Doha community. Our enrolment process is designed to be fully transparent and supportive, with our dedicated Admissions Team guiding you at every point.

To formally begin, kindly complete the application form provided below. Once the submission is complete, our team will conduct an initial review and contact you promptly to guide you through the subsequent steps. For easy reference, detailed information is included at the bottom of this page. Should you require immediate support, please contact our Admissions Office at +974 4496 5888.

Complete the Application Form

The secure form opens on the official King’s College Doha website.

Application Requirements

At King’s College Doha, we thoroughly review every application on an individual basis. We carefully assess academic potential, conduct reports, specific learning needs, age, and nationality to ensure we maintain a truly balanced, inclusive, and diverse learning environment across our campuses.

Required Documents

To process your application, we require the following mandatory documents:

  • One recent passport-size photograph
  • Copy of the student’s birth certificate
  • Copy of the student’s passport
  • Behaviour/attendance reports from the current school
  • Last two annual report cards

Submitting Your Documents

To complete your application, all mandatory documents must be uploaded. If any non-essential documents are unavailable at the time of submission, they may be provided at a later stage.

Additional Information

If you require further guidance on document submission or have any questions, our Admissions Team is happy to assist you.

What year group should my child join?

At King’s College Doha, we deliver an enhanced British curriculum, which provides a rigorous academic programme designed to instill excellence from the earliest years. Our curriculum aligns fully with the UK National Curriculum, preparing students for success at top global universities.

In line with the Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MOEHE) guidelines in Qatar, age-appropriate year placement is determined by a child’s age as of 31 August in the year of entry. Please consult the table below to accurately identify the appropriate year group for your child. Our Admissions Team is also happy to guide you through this process.
