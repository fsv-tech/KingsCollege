Admissions Process

Start Your Journey with King’s College Doha

We are ready to accept your application and warmly welcome your family into the King’s College Doha community. Our dedicated Admissions Team manages the process from start to finish, guaranteeing a seamless, supportive, and highly personalized experience.

Given the high demand and limited availability in certain year groups, we strongly advise families to initiate the admissions process early to secure a place at Qatar’s leading British school.

Easy 5-step Admissions process

1.

Make an Enquiry

Begin by contacting our Admissions Team, who specialize in providing a seamless and highly personalized entry process. They will take the time to deeply understand your child's specific educational needs and expertly guide you through the essential next steps toward joining King’s College Doha.

2.

Visit King’s College Doha

We highly encourage you to experience life at King’s firsthand by booking a personalized tour of our Al Thumama and Mesaimeer campuses. Explore our cutting-edge facilities, observe Outstanding learning in action, and engage with our Leadership Team. Tours are available weekly, providing a true sense of our warm and ambitious school community.

3.

Submit your application

Complete our comprehensive online application form and upload all necessary supporting documents to formalize your request to join King’s College Doha. Should you require any guidance during this stage, our Admissions Team remains readily available to assist you.

4.

Assessment & Offer

Following the receipt of your application, we will schedule an age-appropriate assessment designed to fully understand your child's learning profile and academic potential. For families applying from outside Qatar, convenient remote assessments can be arranged. Assessment outcomes are communicated quickly, and successful applicants will promptly receive a formal offer of a place.

5.

Secure your place

To finalize and confirm your child’s place at King’s College Doha, simply accept the formal offer, return the requested enrolment documents, and complete the necessary registration fee within the specified timeframe. We are excited to welcome your family to our community and begin this exciting educational partnership.
