Accreditation

Accreditations, Partners and Sponsors

Excellence Through Partnership

At King's College Doha, we believe exceptional education is strengthened through trusted partnerships and recognised standards. Our accreditations, sponsors and global affiliations reflect our commitment to delivering an outstanding British education, creating enriching opportunities for every student, and continually striving for excellence. Together, they help us encourage courage, inspire ambition and prepare our students to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Qatar National Schools Accreditation

King's College Doha is proud to hold the highest school accreditation available in Qatar. In May 2022, we were awarded a five-year accreditation as an ‘Outstanding’ school by the Qatar National Schools Accreditation (QNSA).

Established by the State of Qatar, QNSA ensures private schools meet rigorous international standards across teaching and learning, leadership, student outcomes and school operations. A five year accreditation is the highest recognition a school can achieve, reflecting sustained excellence across all areas of school life.

We are honoured that this achievement was also recognised by our partner school, King's College UK, further reinforcing the strength of our shared heritage and commitment to educational excellence.

International and Global Affiliations

As a leading British international school, King's College Doha is committed to collaboration, innovation and continuous improvement. We are an active member of the British Schools in the Middle East (BSME), where our staff contribute to the wider educational community by chairing both the Teaching and Learning and Safeguarding committees.

Our students also benefit from opportunities to develop their confidence, leadership and global perspective through internationally recognised programmes and competitions, including:

  • The Duke of Edinburgh's International Award
  • Model United Nations
  • World Scholar's Cup
  • Pearson Examination Centre

These experiences encourage students to challenge themselves, broaden their horizons and develop the skills they need for success beyond the classroom.

British Chamber of Commerce Qatar

King's College Doha is proud to sponsor the British Chamber of Commerce Qatar (BCCQ), strengthening our connection with the British business community, the British Embassy and leading organisations across Doha.

As the only school to hold this partnership with the BCCQ, we are uniquely positioned to foster meaningful relationships that benefit our school community. The partnership reflects our strong British heritage, trusted reputation and commitment to providing families with an internationally connected education.

Doha Rugby Football Centre

King's College Doha is proud to sponsor the Minis and Youth section of the Doha Rugby Football Centre (DRFC), one of Qatar's longest established community sports organisations.

As DRFC's exclusive education partner, we share a commitment to developing confidence, teamwork, resilience and wellbeing in young people. Together, we create opportunities for children to grow through sport, build lasting friendships and enjoy experiences that extend beyond the classroom, strengthening our connection with families across the wider Doha community.
