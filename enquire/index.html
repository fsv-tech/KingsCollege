Start Your Journey with King’s College Doha

Selecting the right school is arguably the single most important decision for your child’s future, and at King’s College Doha, our team is here to support your family every step of the way. Whether your questions concern our enriched British curriculum, our Outstanding facilities across two campuses, or the streamlined admissions process, we warmly welcome your enquiry. Simply complete the form below, and a dedicated member of our Admissions Team will be in touch to provide the exact information you require.

Why Choose King’s College Doha?

At King’s College Doha, we proudly deliver an Outstanding British education that actively nurtures academic excellence, creativity, and essential character development. With a firm commitment to small class sizes, world-class facilities, and highly qualified specialist teachers, every student receives personalized attention, profound encouragement, and the expert support necessary to flourish. Beyond the classroom, our warm and inclusive community fosters confidence, leadership, and a lifelong love of learning, ensuring our students are fully prepared to thrive both in Qatar and across the wider global stage.

What Happens Next?

Once you submit your initial enquiry, our Admissions Team will promptly contact you to discuss your child’s specific needs and provide clear, detailed answers to any questions you may have. We will then extend an invitation for a personalized school tour if you haven’t already booked one. Following your visit, our team will expertly guide you through the subsequent steps required to formally secure your child’s place at King’s College Doha.
