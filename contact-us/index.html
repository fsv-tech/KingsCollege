We'd love to hear from you

Thank you for your interest in King's College Doha. Our school operates two dedicated campuses: the Al Thumama Campus, home to our Pre-Prep School (Pre-School to Year 2), and the Mesaimeer Campus, which caters to our Prep, Senior, and Sixth Form Schools (Year 3 to Year 13). Whether you’re looking to join our school, explore the admissions process, or book a personalized tour, our dedicated teams are ready to assist you every step of the way.
