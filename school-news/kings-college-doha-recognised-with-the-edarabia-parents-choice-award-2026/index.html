    King’s College Doha Recognised with the Edarabia Parents’ Choice Award 2026

    King’s College Doha is delighted to have been recognised with the Edarabia Parents’ Choice Award 2026, a recognition that reflects the confidence, trust and support of the families at the heart of our school community.

    Unlike awards based on nominations or applications, the Edarabia Parents’ Choice Awards are determined entirely by authentic parent feedback. The awards recognise outstanding K-12 schools through an evaluation of parent votes and written reviews, identifying the Top 10 schools in each city based on the real experiences of their communities. This community-led approach celebrates the schools that consistently earn the confidence and satisfaction of their families.

    For everyone at King’s College Doha, this recognition is especially significant because it comes directly from our parents. Every review, recommendation and vote represents the experiences of families who have entrusted us with their children’s education, and we are deeply grateful for their continued support.

    At King’s, we believe that outstanding education is built on strong partnerships between school and home. Together, we create an environment where every child is encouraged to develop confidence, curiosity and character, supported by exceptional teaching, dedicated pastoral care and a vibrant co-curricular programme. It is this shared commitment that makes our community so special.

    Principal Kate Jackson said:

    “Receiving the Edarabia Parents’ Choice Award is a great honour because it reflects the voices of the families who know our school best. We are incredibly thankful to every parent who took the time to share their experience. This award belongs to our entire community, our pupils, parents, teachers and support staff, whose shared commitment to excellence creates an environment where children are encouraged to thrive every day.”

    As part of the King’s family of schools, we remain committed to providing an exceptional British education that inspires every student to achieve their full potential while remaining grounded in our core values of Community, Courage, Curiosity, Collaboration and Creativity.

    This award is not only a celebration of what we have achieved together, but also an encouragement to continue striving for excellence in everything we do. We would like to extend our thanks to every member of our community for making King’s College Doha such a special place to learn, grow and belong.
